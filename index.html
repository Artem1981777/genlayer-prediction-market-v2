GenLayer Prediction Market Resolver

An Intelligent Contract prediction market on GenLayer Testnet Bradbury: permissionless staking, resolution from cited immutable web sources, a dispute window, settlement, and a phase-gated void().

GitHub repo Contract source Simulation Review response

The fix: phase-gated void()

Steward concern: any account could call void() while a funded market was still open and cancel it before resolution.

Now void() stays permissionless but gates on phase. While the market is open for staking it reverts:

market still open for staking, cannot void before staking_deadline

Post-deadline voids and dispute-phase voids are unchanged; a decided YES/NO market can never be voided.

Contract lifecycle
